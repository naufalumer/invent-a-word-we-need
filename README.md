**resttigue** (n., REST-tee-g): the exhaustion you feel after a whole day of doing absolutely nothing.

English had no word for being tired *because* you rested — tired after a marathon, yes, but tired after a day on the sofa doing nothing at all, somehow more tired than after a busy day. That feeling finally has a name. The blend is transparent (rest + fatigue), it says itself out loud, and it spells the way it sounds.

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=output python3 main.py
```
